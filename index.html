Every serve, every set, every spike, every victory starts here.

Volleyball sessions are happening on every Tuesday and Thursday from 6:00PM to 9:00PM at APU Volleyball Court. Whether you've been playing for years or just a complete beginner that barely know anything about volleyball but is eager to learn, everyone is welcome.

Club History

What started as five friends simply hitting a volleyball in the park has exploded into an official APU community complete with dedicated training facilities and inter-university competitions.

Mission, Vision and Objective

Join a diverse, inclusive community dedicated to loving the game, building lifelong friendships, and winning together.
