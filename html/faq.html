Frequently Asked Question (FAQ)

Who can join the Haikyuu Volleyball Society?

All APU students are welcome to join the club, regardless of their volleyball experience or skill level.

What are the training days?

Training is held every Tuesday and Thursday from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

Do I need volleyball experience to join?

No. Every skill level is welcomed. Beginners are encouraged to join and experienced member are always available to provide guidance and support.

Where are the training sessions conducted?

Training sessions are conducted at the APU Volleyball Court. You can find it beside the APU Open Parking Space.

Do I need to bring my own volleyball?

No. Volleyballs are provided by the club, but members may bring their own if they prefer.

How do I register as a member?

You can register by completing the Registration Form available on the website under the Forms section.

What activities does the club organize?

The club organizes weekly training sessions, friendly matches, tournaments, team-building activities, and social events throughout the academic year.

Who can I contact if I have further questions?

You can submit an enquiry through the Enquiry Form on the website or contact the club committee via the official social media accounts.
