Photo Gallery

A collection of photos capturing the best moments from our club sessions

APU Volleyball Court

The APU Volleyball court is located beside the APU Open Car Park. This is where our club members have our sessions every Tuesday and Thursday, from 6:00PM to 9:00PM.

Lower Body Warm Up

Our sessions always start with a full-body warm-up to reduce the risk of injury. For our lower body, we do pulse raisers, jumping, walking lunges, and side lunges.

Upper Body Warm Up

For our upper body, we do arm circles, shoulder rotations and wrist streches.

Serve Practice

In this photo, our club member is learning how to serve. Serving is performed by the player in the right-back position, standing behind the court's baseline.

Set Practice

Now, our club member move to set practice. The primary goal of a set is to position the ball high enough and accurately in the air so the attacker can jump and spike it into the opponent's court.

Wall Spiking

Unfortunately, this member doesn't know how to jump spike yet. So, he practices the spiking movement by hitting it against a flat, solid wall repeatedly.

Diving

During our friendly match, we captured this incredible diving save by one of our club members. Diving is an emergency defensive technique used to save a ball that is too far away.

Digging Practice

We have digging practice in every session. Digging is one of the most critical defensive training routines in volleyball because it sharpens reaction time and ball control.
