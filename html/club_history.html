Club History

About us

Club Founding

The Haikyuu Volleyball Society initially started out with five friends tossing and hitting a volleyball at the park. At the beginning, it was never about winning trophies. It was more about the process of having fun when playing volleyball with friends.

Club Establishment

As our friend group slowly grew, we decided to acquire permission from APU to establish a new club called Haikyuu Volleyball Club. After acquiring permission from the university council, our club was then established on August 2023. We transitioned from a small friend group playing volleyball at the park to an entire community of volleyball enjoyers playing volleyball together while having fun. APU provided us a volleyball court and training facilities which allows our members to play freely.

The Modern Era

Today, we have club training sessions every Wednesday, Thursday and Friday from 6pm to 9pm. We also regularly host in-club tournaments and occasionally join inter-university competitions. Club members are allowed to freely join any day of the available training sessions.
